\begin{highlights}
\item A cross-plane attack-path model of an automotive plant: IT, identity, cloud, OT, suppliers.
\item Corpus of 20 public automotive manufacturing incidents, 2017--2025, with confidence labels.
\item The likeliest route to a stopped line is 11.6x likelier than any route reaching level 2.
\item Recovery controls dominate expected loss; SaaS and supplier controls are least removable.
\item Partial hardening displaces risk: -73\% espionage loss but +26\% ransomware loss.
\end{highlights}
