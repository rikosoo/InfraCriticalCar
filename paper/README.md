# The paper, and its venue editions

One body of text, several wrappers. `body.tex` and `abstract.tex` hold the
content; each wrapper adds the class, the front matter and the bibliography
style a given venue expects. Change the text once and every edition changes.

| File | Target | Class | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| `main.tex` | IEEE conference | `IEEEtran` (conference) | 14 |
| `paper-ieee-journal.tex` | IEEE OJVT / TVT / Access | `IEEEtran` (journal) | 13 |
| `paper-sae.tex` | SAE Technical Paper / SAE Int. Journal | `article`, single column | 22 |
| `paper-elsevier.tex` | Computers & Security, IJCIP | `elsarticle` preprint | 35 |
| `main-pt.tex` | Portuguese edition (e.g. SBSeg) | `IEEEtran` + `babel` | 14 |

```bash
cd paper
pdflatex main && bibtex main && pdflatex main && pdflatex main   # any of the five
```

All five build with a stock TeX Live (`texlive-latex-base`, `-recommended`,
`-extra`, `texlive-publishers`, `texlive-pictures`, `texlive-science`,
`texlive-lang-portuguese`) and on Overleaf without configuration. Every figure
is TikZ/pgfplots, so there is no image conversion step. As of the last build all
five compile with **zero errors and zero undefined references or citations**.

## How the editions stay consistent

* `preamble.tex` – shared packages, plus the lengths (`\capmpathcol`,
  `\capmvictimcol`, …) and `\capmtablefont` that let one generated table fit a
  two-column IEEE page and a single-column Elsevier page.
* `\capmsinglecolumnfloats` – single-column wrappers call it; it maps
  `table*`/`figure*` onto `table`/`figure` and narrows the wide columns.
* `tables/`, `figures/` (English) and `tables-pt/`, `figures-pt/` (Portuguese)
  are generated by `experiments/build_paper_assets.py` from
  `experiments/results/`. Do not edit them by hand.

## Before submitting

Venue-independent:

1. Fill in author, affiliation, e-mail and acknowledgements in the wrapper.
2. Replace the elicited edge parameters, or document the elicitation protocol
   (who, how many, how disagreement was resolved). This is the first thing a
   journal reviewer will challenge.
3. Refresh the related-work section against the last two years of OT
   attack-graph and manufacturing-security literature.
4. Decide whether to add a baseline comparison (e.g. CVSS-driven or
   MulVAL-style prioritisation) — journals usually ask "better than what?".

Venue-specific:

* **SAE** – SAE issues its own manuscript template after the paper offer;
  transfer the content into it. Keep the Contact Information and Definitions
  sections. SAE requires a signed copyright form and, for most titles, a
  presentation commitment.
* **IEEE journals** – add author biographies, check the title's page limit, and
  for IEEE Access swap in `ieeeaccess.cls` (the body needs no change).
* **Elsevier** – the wrapper already carries Highlights, CRediT, competing
  interest, generative-AI and data-availability statements; complete them.

**One at a time.** Simultaneous submission of the same manuscript to more than
one journal is prohibited by SAE, IEEE and Elsevier alike. These editions exist
so that the next submission is ready the day a decision arrives, not so that
several go out together.
